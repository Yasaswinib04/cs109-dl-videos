These are sample transactions, not yours — they show what the tracker does. Adding your own messages replaces them.

Your messages are parsed on this page and stored with it. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Import transactions

Load a bank or card statement (CSV or Excel) — the most complete source — or paste alert messages. Duplicates are skipped either way, so overlapping imports are safe.

Where to get a statement

  1. ICICI — net banking → Bank Accounts → Statements → pick a date range → download as .xls.
  2. Credit cards — each issuer's net banking or app has a statement download; Excel or CSV beats PDF.
  3. Statements carry a running balance, so this page can check every row adds up before it imports anything.
  4. Messages still work: paste them above, or load an sms-….xml from SMS Backup & Restore.
